**Study notes**
- Know whether *your* airplane is certificated for flight into known icing (FIKI). If not, any forecast icing along the route becomes a no-go or reroute discussion — tie this to Task I.B (icing products) and I.C.S5.
- Pitot heat is the one "anti-ice" system nearly every IFR airplane has; a blocked pitot tube/drain/static port scenario (IFH Ch 3) is a favorite oral topic.

**Study notes**
- **VOR check (91.171):** within preceding **30 days** — VOT ±4°, ground checkpoint ±4°, airborne checkpoint ±6°, dual VOR cross-check 4° between each other, or airway centerline check. Log **date, place, bearing error, signature** (DPBS/"date-place-bearing-signature").
- **Inspections stack ("AV1ATE"):** Annual (91.409), VOR 30 days if VOR used (91.171), 100-hour if for hire (91.409(b)), Altimeter/pitot-static 24 mo (91.411), Transponder 24 mo (91.413), ELT 12 mo (91.207).
- GPS approach authorization: a non-WAAS TSO-C129 unit needs RAIM (predictive RAIM check for the ETA — AIM 1-1-17) and current database; WAAS (TSO-C145/146) removes the RAIM-prediction requirement in most cases (AIM 1-1-18).

## Task C. Instrument Flight Deck Check

**ACS references:** 14 CFR part 91; AC 91.21-1; FAA-H-8083-2, -3, -15, -25; POH/AFM
**Objective:** Preflight check of the instruments necessary for IFR flight.

| ACS Element | Element summary | 14 CFR (FARs) | AIM | IFH 8083-15B | Other refs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| IR.II.C.K1 | Purpose of the instrument flight deck check; detecting defects | 91.7 (airworthiness; PIC discontinues flight) | — | **Ch 3** ("Instrument Systems Preflight Procedures" — taxi checks: TC, HI, AI, compass, altimeter within 75 ft) | POH checklist |
| IR.II.C.K2 | IFR airworthiness: inspections & required equipment | 91.205(d) (IFR equipment — "GRABCARD"); 91.171 (VOR check); 91.409 (annual/100-hr); 91.411 (pitot-static); 91.413 (transponder); 91.207 (ELT); 91.203 (documents — "ARROW") | 1-1-17(b) (GPS database currency) | Ch 3 | POH/AFM equipment list |
| IR.II.C.K3 | Procedures, documentation, and limits for inoperative equipment | **91.213** (MEL or the 91.213(d) deactivate-and-placard flow); 91.405 (discrepancy records); 91.7 | — | — | POH KOEL; AC 91-67 |
| IR.II.C.R1 | Operating with inoperative equipment | 91.213; 91.205(d) | — | — | — |
| IR.II.C.R2 | Outdated navigation publications or databases | 91.103 | 1-1-17(b); 5-1-3 (NOTAMs) | Ch 7 | AC 90-100A (expired database procedures) |
| IR.II.C.S1 | Perform the preflight inspection per checklist; judge fitness for instrument flight | 91.7 | — | Ch 3 | POH/AFM |

**Study notes**
- **91.205(d) IFR equipment — "GRABCARD":** **G**enerator/alternator, **R**adios (two-way comm & nav appropriate to route), **A**ttitude indicator, **B**all (slip-skid), **C**lock (sweep second or digital), **A**ltimeter (sensitive/adjustable), **R**ate of turn, **D**irectional gyro — plus all day/night VFR equipment.
- Inop equipment flow (no MEL, 91.213(d)): not required by 91.205 / type design / ADs / POH equipment list? → deactivate or remove, **placard "INOPERATIVE"**, log it, and the PIC (or mechanic) determines it's not a hazard.
- Instrument taxi check (IFH Ch 3): turn indicator shows the turn (ball opposite), heading indicator/compass move freely and agree, attitude indicator stays erect (≤5° tilt), altimeter within **75 ft** of field elevation.
