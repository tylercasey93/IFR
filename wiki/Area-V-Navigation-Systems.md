**Study notes**
- **DME arc technique (IFH Ch 7):** lead the turn onto the arc (≈0.5 NM at 150 kts GS), then "turn 10, twist 10" — keep the reference radial moving, shallow corrections toward/away from the station to hold ±1 NM.
- 91.187 + AIM 5-3-3: under IFR in controlled airspace you must report the loss of VOR, TACAN, ADF, GPS anomalies, or ILS capability — along with aircraft ID, degree of impairment, and assistance desired.

**Study notes**
- **ODP vs. SID (AIM 5-2-9):** ODPs exist for obstacle clearance and may be flown without an ATC clearance at non-towered fields (part 91); SIDs are ATC-assigned for traffic flow and require the clearance. Both assume crossing the departure end at ≥35 ft, reaching 400 ft AGL before turning, and **≥200 ft/NM** climb unless a higher gradient is published.
- **"Descend via"** a STAR (AIM 5-4-1) authorizes both the lateral path and the published altitude/speed constraints — know what "except maintain" modifies.
- Mandatory IFR reports (91.183 + AIM 5-3-3, "MARVELOUS VFR C500" if you like mnemonics): missed approach, airspeed ±5%/10 kts change, reaching a holding fix, VFR-on-top altitude changes, ETA changes ±3 min (non-radar), leaving a holding fix, outer marker inbound (non-radar), unforecast weather, safety of flight issues, vacating an altitude, final approach fix inbound (non-radar), radio/nav failure, climb/descent rate falls below 500 fpm.
