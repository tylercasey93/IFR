## Task A. Loss of Communications

**ACS references:** 14 CFR part 91; AIM; FAA-H-8083-2, -3, -15, -16, -25
**Objective:** Loss of communications while operating solely by reference to instruments.

**Study notes**
- **Route priority (91.185(c)(1)) — "AVE F":** **A**ssigned route → if being **V**ectored, direct to the fix/route in the vector clearance → in the absence of both, the route ATC said to **E**xpect → else the **F**iled route.
- **Altitude (91.185(c)(2)) — "MEA," highest of, for each segment:** the **M**inimum IFR altitude, the **E**xpected altitude, or the **A**ssigned altitude.
- **VFR conditions after the failure (91.185(b)):** continue VFR and land as soon as **practicable** — not "as soon as possible." Then notify ATC.
- Squawk **7600** (AIM 6-4-2, 6-2-2).

## Task B. One Engine Inoperative (Simulated) During Straight-and-Level Flight and Turns (AMEL, AMES)

**ACS references:** FAA-H-8083-2, -3, -15, -25; POH/AFM
**Objective:** Flight solely by reference to instruments with one engine inoperative. *(Multiengine applicants only — see ACS Appendix 2, Safety of Flight.)*

**Study notes**
- Memory flow: **maintain control → mixtures/props/throttles forward → flaps & gear up → identify (dead foot) → verify (retard that throttle) → feather (simulated) → secure**. On instruments, do it all while keeping the scan going — bank ~2–3° into the good engine, half-ball deflection toward the operating engine.
- **Blue line (Vyse)** is the working speed for everything single-engine; know your zero-sideslip technique and why the evaluator sets zero thrust rather than actually feathering.

## Task D. Approach with Loss of Primary Flight Instrument Indicators

**ACS references:** 14 CFR part 91; FAA-H-8083-2, -3, -15, -16, -25; POH/AFM; Terminal Procedures Publications
**Objective:** A non-precision approach solely by reference to instruments with loss of primary flight instruments ("partial panel").

| ACS Element | Element summary | 14 CFR (FARs) | AIM | IFH 8083-15B | Other refs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| IR.VII.D.K1 | Recognizing inaccurate/inoperative primary instruments; advising ATC | **91.187** (report malfunctions of nav/approach/comm equipment under IFR in controlled airspace) | 5-3-3 (additional reports); 6-1-2 | **Ch 11 (partial panel)**; Ch 3 (failure indications) | POH annunciations |
| IR.VII.D.K2 | Failure modes of primary instruments; correcting/minimizing the loss | — | 7-2 (altimeter errors) | **Ch 3** (pitot-static blockages, vacuum failure, ADC/AHRS failure modes); Ch 11 | Advanced Avionics Handbook (display reversion) |
| IR.VII.D.R1 | Using secondary/standby displays after a primary failure | — | — | Ch 3; Ch 11 | POH (standby instruments, reversionary mode) |
| IR.VII.D.R2 | Maintaining aircraft control | — | — | Ch 5 (partial-panel technique: timed turns, compass turns) | — |
| IR.VII.D.R3 | Distraction, task prioritization, loss of SA, disorientation | — | 8-1-5 (a real disorientation trap with a failed AI) | Ch 1 | — |
| IR.VII.D.S1 | Advise ATC/evaluator if unable to comply | 91.187; 91.123(a) | 5-3-3 | Ch 11 | — |
| IR.VII.D.S2 | Complete a non-precision approach without primary flight instruments (per Task VI.A skills) | 91.175 | 5-4 | Ch 10; Ch 11 | See [Area VI, Task A](Area-VI-Instrument-Approach-Procedures.md#task-a-non-precision-approach) |
| IR.VII.D.S3 | SRM/CRM | — | — | Ch 1 | — |

**Study notes**
- Classic failure diagnosis (IFH Ch 3): **vacuum failure** → AI and HI slowly tumble while pitot-static instruments agree; **blocked static** → altimeter freezes, VSI zeros, ASI errs with altitude; **blocked pitot (ram + drain)** → ASI acts like an altimeter. Say what failed, cover it, and switch to the composite scan.
- Partial-panel turns: **timed turns** (standard rate: 3°/sec) and **magnetic compass turns** (UNOS — undershoot north, overshoot south by ~latitude degrees) are in IFH Ch 5.
