**Study notes**
- Copy clearances with **CRAFT**: **C**learance limit, **R**oute, **A**ltitude, **F**requency, **T**ransponder (AIM 4-4-3 lists the same order of items).
- A **clearance void time** (AIM 5-2-7) applies departing a non-towered field: not off by the void time → your clearance is void, and you must notify ATC within 30 minutes of the void time or search-and-rescue procedures begin.
- 91.123: you may deviate from a clearance only with an amended clearance, in an emergency, or in response to a TCAS RA — and you must report any emergency deviation to ATC as soon as possible.

**Study notes**
- **Entries (AIM 5-3-8):** relative to the holding course, sector method — **direct** (~180° sector), **parallel** (~110°), **teardrop** (~70°). Entries are *recommended*, not regulatory, but the ACS expects an appropriate one.
- **Standard pattern** = right turns; "nonstandard" (left turns) will be stated in the clearance or charted. An ATC holding clearance for an unpublished hold must include: direction from the fix, fix name, radial/course/bearing, leg length if DME/RNAV, direction of turns if nonstandard, and EFC.
- Report to ATC: time and altitude **reaching** the holding fix, and when **leaving** it (AIM 5-3-3) — plus "unable EFC with fuel" the moment the fuel math stops working.
