# Area of Operation VI — Instrument Approach Procedures

[← Area V](Area-V-Navigation-Systems.md) · [Wiki Home](README.md) · [Next: Area VII →](Area-VII-Emergency-Operations.md)

The regulatory spine of this whole Area is **14 CFR 91.175** (takeoff and landing under IFR): when you may descend below DA/MDA, the required visual references, and the missed approach mandate. Approach procedures themselves are regulatory via **part 97**. The AIM's arrival section (5-4) and the IPH Ch 4 explain how to fly them; IFH Ch 10 ties it together for the single pilot.

**Study notes**
- **91.175(c) visual references** (any one, plus required flight visibility): approach light system (but only down to 100 ft above TDZE on the ALS alone unless the red terminating/side-row bars are visible), threshold, threshold markings/lights, REIL, VASI/PAPI, touchdown zone or its markings/lights, runway or its markings/lights.
- **MDA is a floor** (+100/−0 in the ACS): without vertical guidance you may not descend below it even momentarily until the 91.175(c) triad is satisfied.
- A **VDP** is the point on final from which a normal descent from MDA to touchdown can begin; past the VDP without visual contact, plan the miss even though the MAP is still ahead.

**Study notes**
- **DA vs. MDA:** a DA is a *decision* altitude — the miss is begun at DA and some altitude loss during the transition is expected and protected; an MDA may not be penetrated at all without 91.175(c) references.
- Standard **CAT I ILS minimums**: 200 ft DH / ½ SM (or RVR 1800–2400 depending on lighting). Localizer course is 3–6° wide (tailored for ~700 ft at threshold) — roughly 4× as sensitive as a VOR.
- **LPV** approaches (AC 90-107, AIM 5-4-5) are *approaches with vertical guidance* (APV), flown to a DA exactly like an ILS; the ACS permits an LPV with a DA ≤ 300 ft HAT to count as the precision approach demonstration (see ACS Appendix 3).

**Study notes**
- Going missed **before the MAP** (AIM 5-4-21): continue *laterally* along the final approach course to the MAP, then fly the published miss; climbing early is fine, **turning early is not** — obstacle protection assumes the turn starts at the MAP.
- The published missed approach obstacle clearance assumes a **200 ft/NM** climb unless a higher gradient is charted.

**Study notes**
- If the maneuvering speed for the circle exceeds your category's ceiling, use the **higher category's minimums** (97.3 / AIM 5-4-20). Newer charts with the inverse "C" icon use **expanded circling radii** — know which your chart uses.
- Stay at or above the circling MDA until in a position for a normal descent to landing (91.175(c) still applies while circling).

**Study notes**
- The 100-ft ALS rule: descending from DA/MDA on the **approach lights alone**, you may only go to **100 ft above TDZE** unless the **red terminating bars or red side-row bars** are distinctly visible (91.175(c)(3)(i)).
- Landing illusions (AIM 8-1-5 / IFH Ch 1): wide runway → feel high (land hard); narrow/upsloping runway → feel high (approach low). Brief the expected picture before breaking out.
