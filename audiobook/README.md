# Cleared as Filed

*A fake-dating charter-aviation romance that teaches the Instrument Rating ACS.*

Captain Wren Hale and First Officer Theo Marsh fly a King Air 350 for a struggling Part 135 charter operator in Seattle. When their best client mistakes a flight-deck argument for a lovers' quarrel — and makes "the couple crew" a condition of the contract that keeps the company alive — they agree to fake it for one season. Twenty chapters, one full winter of instrument flying, and every Area of Operation in the Instrument Rating ACS later, nobody is faking anything.

Every chapter teaches one area of the ACS through the flying itself, and ends with a **Crew Debrief** — an in-story recap of the chapter's rules and mnemonics. The romance beats deliberately mirror the aviation concepts (currency vs. proficiency, decision altitude, holding without an EFC, lost communications, the missed approach), so remembering the story *is* remembering the material. Companion reference: the [ACS Correlation Wiki](../wiki/README.md).

## Producing the audio (narration-ready workflow)

The chapters are written for single-narrator audio: POV named in each chapter's first line, dialogue attributed frequently, scene breaks marked with `---`, and numbers/regulations spelled out as spoken in dialogue ("ninety-one one eighty-five").

**Recommended workflow:**

1. **Pick a TTS service.** Best quality first: ElevenLabs (paste or upload each chapter; one narrator voice; "Narrative" style), Speechify, or Amazon Polly long-form. Free/local: Apple's Live Speech / "Speak Screen" on iOS or `say` on macOS reading the markdown files.
2. **One file per chapter.** Feed each `Chapter-NN-*.md` separately; name outputs `NN-title.mp3` so any podcast/audiobook app plays them in order. Strip the `#` heading line or let the narrator read it as the chapter title — both work.
3. **Sideload:** drop the MP3s into a podcast app (Overcast, Pocket Casts), the Apple Books audiobook shelf, or a "private podcast" feed to get resume/speed controls while studying.

### Content notes

- Heat level: warm mainstream romance. Chapters 13 and 19 contain tasteful open-door scenes (no explicit content); all other chapters are banter and kissing.
- The aviation content is written to match the current ACS/AIM/FAR editions cited in the [wiki](../wiki/README.md) — but this is a novel, not a study guide of record. Where the story and the regs ever disagree, trust the regs, and check the wiki for the citation.
